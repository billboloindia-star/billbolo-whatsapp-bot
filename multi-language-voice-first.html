How It Works

Not just understood — written back the same way

Plenty of tools can understand a Hindi or Tamil voice note. BillBolo goes further: the quotation or invoice it generates has the item names and client name written in that same language and script — not translated into English, not transliterated into Latin letters. Headers like ITEM, QTY, and GRAND TOTAL stay in clean English so the document still reads like a proper business invoice, with your content exactly as you said it.

No menus to navigate, no language setting to find — just speak or type naturally, in whichever language is comfortable.

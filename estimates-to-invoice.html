Before You Start Over

Should You Re-Create the Invoice From Scratch?

Copying details from an old quotation into a new invoice is a start — but here's where it goes wrong.

✕ Starting from scratch

  • Re-typing the same client, items and rates all over again
  • Easy to make a mistake copying numbers by hand
  • No link between the original quotation and the final invoice
  • Forgetting to add a due date or payment mode
  • No way to tell which quotations are still open vs. already billed
  • Discounts and GST details have to be re-entered too

✓ With BillBolo

  • Just say "convert Sharma's quotation to invoice" — done
  • Client, items and totals carried over exactly, no retyping
  • The invoice references the exact quotation it came from
  • Add a due date and payment mode in the same message
  • Converted quotations are marked so they're never reused by mistake
  • Discounts and GST carry over automatically

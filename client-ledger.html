How It Works

No ledger book, no spreadsheet — just WhatsApp

Most small businesses in India still track udhaar in a notebook, or trust memory for who owes what. BillBolo turns that into a text message: say who owes you money and why, and it's logged. Ask who owes you, and you get a straight answer — no flipping through pages or scrolling old chats.

It understands plain language — English, Hindi, Hinglish, or a mix — so there's no fixed command format to learn.

Before You Reach for a Notebook

Should You Track Udhaar by Hand?

A notebook or memory is a start — but here's what it can't do.

✕ Tracking it yourself

  • Relying on memory or a notebook that can be lost or damaged
  • No single place to see everyone who owes you, at a glance
  • Easy to forget to note down a smaller udhaar entry
  • Awkward to double-check "did they already pay me back?"
  • No record of what the money was actually for
  • Nothing to show a client if there's a dispute later

✓ With BillBolo

  • Just text it — "Ramesh owes me 2000 for tiling work"
  • Ask "who owes me money" anytime for the full picture
  • Every entry saved with what it was for and when
  • "Mark Ramesh as paid" clears the balance instantly
  • Works in English, Hindi, or a mix — however you naturally type
  • Nothing to install, nothing to open except WhatsApp

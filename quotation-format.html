What It Is

What is a Quotation Format?

A quotation is what a business sends a potential customer before any work starts or money changes hands — it lays out exactly what they'll get and what it'll cost. It's an offer, not a bill. The invoice comes later, only once the customer has agreed and the job is actually done.

For GST-registered businesses in India, a proper quotation also breaks out the applicable tax — CGST and SGST for work within the state, IGST for interstate — so there are no surprises on the final number later.

Quick Guide

How to Use a Quotation Format

Six steps to fill one out properly — or skip straight to the shortcut at the end.

1

Download the Right Format

Pick PDF, Word, or Excel based on what you need. Excel auto-calculates totals; PDF gives a clean, final document.

2

Fill in Your Business Details

Add your business name, address, phone and GSTIN at the top so the client knows exactly who's quoting.

3

Add Client Information

Enter the client's name, address and contact number. Add their GSTIN too if they're GST-registered.

4

List the Items or Services

One line per item, each with its own quantity, rate and amount. A lump-sum "various charges" line makes clients nervous — specifics build trust.

5

Add GST and Calculate the Total

Apply the correct GST rate per item. Our Excel format calculates this automatically — double check the final total before sending.

6

Add Validity, Terms and Send

Note how long the price holds and any special terms. Save as PDF and share via WhatsApp or email.

What Should a Quotation Format Include?

Six parts every quotation needs — miss one and it can cost you the deal.

1

Your Business Details

Business name, address, phone and GSTIN — so the client knows exactly who's quoting.

2

Client Details

Name, address and contact — always address a specific person, not just "customer."

3

Quotation Number & Date

A unique reference makes it easy to track and follow up later.

4

Validity Period

How long the price holds — typically 7 to 30 days, since costs change.

5

Itemised List

Each item with quantity, rate and amount — never a single lump sum.

6

Tax Details & Total

GST shown separately, then the final total — clearly, before any discount.

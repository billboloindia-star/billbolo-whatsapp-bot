Before You Reach for a Calculator

Should You Work Out Discounts by Hand?

Mental math or a calculator app is a start — but here's where it goes wrong.

✕ Working it out yourself

  • Easy to apply a discount to the wrong line by mistake
  • Forgetting to discount before calculating GST, not after
  • No clean way to show the discount on a printed quotation
  • Re-typing the whole bill if you add or change a discount
  • Mixing up a flat rupee discount with a percentage mid-calculation
  • Client questions the total with no clear breakdown to show them

✓ With BillBolo

  • Say it naturally — per item or for the whole job, BillBolo tells them apart
  • Discount is always applied before GST, correctly, every time
  • Shows up as a clear line on the PDF — nothing hidden in the total
  • Change your mind? Just describe the job again with the new discount
  • Handles both flat rupee amounts and percentages, whichever you say
  • A clean, itemized breakdown your client can actually read
